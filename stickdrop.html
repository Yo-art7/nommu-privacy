Stick Drop Privacy Policy

Effective 5 October 2026

Stick Drop is a single-player mobile game. This policy explains what the app does with information about you. The short version: it does not collect any.

Information the app collects

None. Stick Drop does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal or usage data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no third-party code that tracks you.

Information kept on your device

The app saves your best score, your chosen colour theme and your sound setting. These stay on your phone, inside the app's own storage. They are never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.

Internet and permissions

Stick Drop does not use the internet. Its only permission is vibration, used for short buzzes when you hit or miss. You can turn sound and vibration off in the game.

Sharing your score

If you tap "Share score", Android's own share menu opens with a short text about your score. You choose where it goes. The app itself sends nothing.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
