Privacy Policy for Nommu

Last updated: 11 August 2026

Nommu is a game made by an independent developer. This policy explains, plainly, what the app does with your information. The short version: nothing at all.

What Nommu collects

Nothing. The app does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information. There is no account to create, no sign-in, no email address requested and no analytics.

What Nommu sends over the internet

Nothing. The app requests no network permissions whatsoever. It cannot connect to the internet even if it wanted to. Everything in the game runs on your device and stays there.

What is stored on your device

  • Your best score, and whether the sound is muted.

These are kept in the app's own private storage on your phone. They are never sent anywhere. Uninstalling Nommu deletes them.

The motion sensor

Nommu reads your phone's tilt in order to steer the character. This reading is used for that instant and then discarded. It is not recorded, stored or transmitted. Android does not require a permission for this, and Nommu does not ask for one.

Advertising

This version of Nommu contains no advertising and no advertising software. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated before the version containing adverts is published, and the app's Google Play listing will say that it contains ads.

Children

Nommu is intended for players aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age, because it does not collect information at all.

Your rights

Because no data about you is ever collected, there is nothing held about you to access, correct, export or delete. Removing the app removes everything it saved.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new date at the top.
